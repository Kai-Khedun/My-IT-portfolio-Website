About

I'm Kai. I studied marketing, then computer science, and now I build the part of the product nobody sees.

My first degree was a BCom in Marketing. It taught me to start with the customer: who's ordering, what they expect to happen when they press the button, and what brings them back. I then went back for a degree in Computer Science, which I finished in 2026, and I've completed Google's IT Support certificate too.

Most of my work sits behind the screen. I design schemas, write APIs, set up auth and build the pipelines that test and ship every change. I like rules that live in the database, where nobody can skip them, and a main branch nobody can push to without a review.

This year I led three team builds with the same crew of four, including a full ordering, booking and shop platform for a real restaurant in Durban. I also tutor other students and put together setup kits so they can start coding on day one.

DWG 03.1 · Client project

Capri Pizza Kitchen

Ordering, bookings and a pro shop for a Durban restaurant

Backend lead · team lead
Jun – Nov 2026

Capri is a family-run wood-fire pizza and burger spot in Malvern, Durban, with a pool club and a pro shop under one roof. We built one platform for all three: a website, an iOS and Android app, and a dashboard the owner runs himself. I built the entire backend.

  • One backend shared by the website, the mobile app and the owner dashboard
  • A hybrid data model: 13 PostgreSQL tables for anything that decides price or availability, 4 Firestore collections for carts, live order tracking and logs
  • Rules enforced by the database itself: no double-booked pool tables, no overselling in the shop, and customers only ever see their own orders
  • CI/CD on GitHub Actions. Every change goes through a pull request and a green pipeline before it reaches main
  • Led the team of four and handled the build with the client
